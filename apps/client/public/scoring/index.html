Hong Kong mahjong scoring (faan)

Your hand is worth the sum of the faan of every pattern it matches, and the game can require a minimum faan to declare a win. These are the patterns Modern Mahjong scores, taken straight from its rules engine.

Winning conditions

PatternFaanWhen it applies
自摸 Self-draw1Win on a tile you drew yourself rather than on an opponent’s discard.
門前清 Concealed hand1No claimed melds — the entire hand stayed in your concealed tiles, regardless of self-draw or ron.
槓上開花 Kong replacement2Self-draw the replacement tile right after declaring a gang.
槓上槓 Double kong replacement9Self-draw the replacement of a second gang declared back-to-back, without an intervening discard.
海底撈月 Last tile1Win on the very last live-wall draw or discard before the hand would have run out of tiles.
搶槓 Robbing the kong1Ron an opponent’s 4th tile before it lands in their promoted gang. Only promoted-from-peng gangs are robbable.
平和 All sequences1Four sequences plus a pair that isn’t a yakuhai (no dragons, prevailing wind, or seat wind).

Hand composition

PatternFaanWhen it applies
混一色 Half flush3One suit plus honors only — no tiles from the other two suits.
清一色 Full flush7Every tile from a single suit — no honors, no other suits.
九蓮寶燈 Nine gates13A fully-concealed 1112345678999 of one suit, plus any one extra of the same suit. The 9-tile wait makes this one of the rarest hands in the game.
混么九 Mixed terminals & honors4Every tile is a 1, a 9, or an honor. Mixing terminals with at least one honor.
清么九 All terminals13Only 1s and 9s — no honors, no middle tiles.
字一色 All honors10Every tile is a wind or dragon — no suit tiles at all.

Honors

PatternFaanWhen it applies
三元牌 Dragon triplet1A triplet (or gang) of any dragon — 中, 發, or 白. Stacks per dragon and on top of 大三元 / 小三元.
小三元 Small three dragons5Two dragon triplets plus a pair of the third dragon. Each dragon triplet still adds its own +1.
大三元 Big three dragons8Triplets of all three dragons. Each dragon triplet still adds its own +1, so the total yield is +11.
圈風 Prevailing-wind triplet1A triplet of the prevailing (round) wind. Stacks with 門風 when seat wind is also the prevailing wind.
門風 Seat-wind triplet1A triplet of your own seat wind. When seat wind matches the prevailing wind, both fire (+2 total).
小四喜 Small four winds6Three wind triplets plus a pair of the fourth wind.
大四喜 Big four winds13Triplets of all four winds plus any pair.

Special shapes

PatternFaanWhen it applies
對對和 All triplets3Four triplets (or gangs) plus a pair — no sequences. Suppressed when stronger triplet patterns like 字一色 fire.
七對子 Seven pairs4Seven distinct pairs in a fully-concealed hand. Replaces the standard 4-sets-and-a-pair shape.
十三幺 Thirteen orphans13One of each terminal (1m, 9m, 1p, 9p, 1s, 9s) and each honor (E, S, W, N, 中, 發, 白), with one of them paired up.
四暗刻 All concealed triplets8Four concealed triplets plus a pair. No exposed melds, and on a ron win the discard must complete the pair (otherwise only 三暗刻).
四槓子 All gangs13Four gangs (any mix of concealed / exposed / promoted) plus a pair.

Blessings

PatternFaanWhen it applies
天糊 Blessing of heaven13Dealer wins on the opening 14-tile self-draw — before anyone has discarded.
地糊 Blessing of earth13Non-dealer wins on the dealer’s very first discard — before any other tile has been pitched.
人糊 Blessing of man13Non-dealer wins on their own first self-draw — before they’ve discarded anything.
