How to play Hong Kong mahjong

Hong Kong mahjong is a four-player game. Each player tries to be first to complete a winning hand of four sets and a pair (14 tiles).

The tiles

There are 136 tiles: three suits (characters, dots, bamboo) numbered 1–9, four winds (East, South, West, North) and three dragons (Red, Green, White), four copies of each. Hong Kong rules use no flowers or seasons here.

A turn

On your turn you draw a tile, then discard one. If you hold a winning hand after drawing, you win by self-draw (自摸).

Sets

  • Chi (吃): a run of three in one suit, e.g. 4-5-6 dots. Only from the player on your left.
  • Peng (碰): three identical tiles. From any player.
  • Gang (槓): four identical tiles. You draw a replacement tile afterwards.

Claiming another player's discard exposes that set to the table. Claiming nothing keeps your hand concealed, which scores extra.

Winning

You win on your own draw, or by taking another player's discard (ron, 出銃). The hand must have enough faan to meet the table minimum. More faan means a bigger payout.
